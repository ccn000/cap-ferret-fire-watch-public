Definitive current situation

Full assessment and confidence

Latest new information · 25 Jul 2026, 10:40

DeterioratingOpen assessmentClose assessment

At 03:32 and 03:58, the warning became a set of evacuation orders. The prefect ordered five municipalities evacuated preventively and ordered partial evacuations in the outer-ring sectors of Merignac and Eysines. People were directed by road toward Bordeaux, with reception at Hall 3 of the Parc des Expositions. At 04:09, Le Monde reported that 42 firefighters had been treated, including nine evacuated, all in relative emergencies. At 06:17, Le Monde’s live index said 500 additional military personnel and 1.5 million FFP2 masks are being sent to Gironde. At 06:30, Le Monde described firefighters as overwhelmed by an uncontrolled fire that has already caused about 140,000 evacuations and burned nearly 20,000 hectares. By 09:55, Bruges town hall was also showing an FR-Alert evacuation notice for Bruges extra-rocade. At 10:40, Le Monde’s live index reported that the government wants to deploy a military A400M for the Gironde fire as soon as Saturday, adding potential retardant-drop capacity but not proving the aircraft is already active over the fire. The whole Cap Ferret peninsula remains evacuated and inbound road access remains unavailable. The maritime prefecture has partly reopened Arcachon Bay away from the fire, but the west-coast corridor and embarkation points remain restricted, evacuation boats are suspended, and this is not a public route to Cap Ferret. Bordeaux Airport’s official board is still listing flights and says airport activity continues, but tram and bus service is interrupted between Merignac Soleil and the airport.

What matters now
  • Additional Bordeaux-area municipalities and extra-rocade sectors remain under preventive evacuation orders; Bruges extra-rocade is now also listed by its town hall.
  • Le Monde reports that the government wants to deploy a military A400M for the Gironde fire as soon as Saturday; this is a response-resource signal, not confirmation of active aircraft operations.
  • Le Monde now reports 42 firefighters treated, including nine evacuated, all in relative emergencies.
  • Le Monde now reports nearly 20,000 hectares burned and about 140,000 evacuations, while describing firefighters as overwhelmed by the uncontrolled fire.
  • Road entry to Cap Ferret remains unavailable. Arcachon Bay is partly reopened away from the fire, but restricted west-coast/embarkation zones and the peninsula evacuation mean there is still no viable public-water route for a holiday trip.
  • Bordeaux Airport is still operating flights, but its official board says tram and bus service is interrupted between Merignac Soleil and the airport; verify the airport, airline and ground route before relying on any flight.
Assessment confidence

High confidence in the additional metropolitan evacuation orders and sectors: the prefecture confirms Le Haillan, Eysines extra-rocade and Merignac extra-rocade, Bruges town hall confirms Bruges extra-rocade, and Le Monde reports the broader seven-commune/sector evacuation picture. The exact operational boundary around the airport site still has not been established here. The airport board remains active and explicitly says activity continues, so the dashboard does not declare the airport closed; it treats ground access as disrupted because the same official board says tram and bus service to the airport is interrupted.
