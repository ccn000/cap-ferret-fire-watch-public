Current assessment

What we think now and why

Latest new information · 29 Jul 2026, 09:04

Stabilised after a calmer night, but still volatile under heat riskOpen assessmentClose assessment

The latest material change is that the overnight fire behaviour appears calmer: Le Monde’s morning live index says the Gironde prefecture reported another calm night and a megafire “still stabilised”. That matters because it is the first genuinely newer synthesis point after yesterday’s Lacanau tourist-accommodation evacuation. But it is not a decisive improvement: the fire is still not described as fixed or controlled, Saumos remains a front-line base against recurring flare-ups in the Bordeaux fire zone, and renewed heatwave conditions are now the main risk factor. The practical implication for Cap Ferret and the surrounding coast is still watch-and-wait: no normal tourist reopening signal, no clear relaxation of the regional risk picture, and continued dependence on official access and evacuation orders.

What matters now
  • Newest material update: the prefecture reports another calm night and the Gironde megafire still stabilised.
  • Why this matters: overnight behaviour looks better than yesterday’s flare-up/evacuation picture, but the fire is still not fixed or controlled.
  • Main risk today: renewed heatwave conditions could drive further reprises and complicate firefighting around Bordeaux, Saumos, Lacanau and the wider Gironde coast.
  • Operational signal: Saumos is still being described as a forward base against recurring flare-ups, so the response posture remains active rather than demobilising.
  • Cap Ferret-specific implication: this is still not a tourist-return or reopening signal; access and evacuation decisions remain controlled by official orders.
  • Regional access remains important: road, rail, smoke, airport and evacuation-corridor updates can still matter even if the peninsula-specific status is unchanged.
  • Next important signal to watch: an official move from stabilised to fixed/controlled, plus staged re-entry guidance for residents and then normal visitor access.
Assessment confidence

High confidence that the “calm night / still stabilised” signal is material because it is attributed to the Gironde prefecture in Le Monde’s current live coverage. High confidence that it is not yet a reopening signal because the same source says the megafire is still only stabilised, while other current reporting continues to stress flare-ups and heatwave risk. Moderate confidence on the exact operational trend because official detailed morning figures still need to be reconciled against the next prefectural point.
