Definitive current situation

Do not travel to Cap Ferret: the whole peninsula remains evacuated and the fire is still progressing.

Latest new information · 24 Jul 2026, 18:36

Deteriorating

The decisive facts are the complete evacuation of the Cap Ferret peninsula, a fire still described as uncontrolled and advancing, and the expanded emergency response. Reuters reports the prefecture’s 14:00 briefing put the fire at more than 12,500 hectares and 44,000 evacuations over 48 hours; about 1,000 firefighters are engaged. Authorities report 53 homes and a campsite destroyed. Injury reporting conflicts: the earlier prefectural/SDIS briefing reported 27 firefighters treated, six hospitalised, while a later local report gives a higher figure that is not independently corroborated. Reuters also lists Arès and Andernos for preventive evacuation, and the maritime prefecture has temporarily prohibited navigation on Arcachon Bay. Inbound road access remains closed to the public.

What matters now
  • The entire peninsula remains evacuated; Reuters also lists Arès and Andernos for preventive evacuation on the northern side of the bay.
  • The fire remains uncontrolled; 27 firefighters have required treatment and forecast westerly gusts could reach 80 km/h.
  • Road entry and ordinary navigation on Arcachon Bay are unavailable, so there is no viable land or public-water route for a holiday trip.
  • The next meaningful threshold is official control of the fire and permission to enter, followed by road, ferry, utility, beach, business and forest-access reopening.
Assessment confidence

High confidence on the evacuation and navigation restrictions, the approximately 1,000-firefighter deployment, the prefecture-attributed 12,500-hectare / 44,000-evacuation update, and the absence of a viable travel signal. The clearest corroborated damage count remains 53 homes and one campsite destroyed, with 2,000 homes saved. Treat the conflicting later firefighter-injury count as unconfirmed; earlier wording that 80 homes were burned or affected should not be read as 80 confirmed total destructions.
