Definitive current situation

Full assessment and confidence

Latest new information · 25 Jul 2026, 11:57

DeterioratingOpen assessmentClose assessment

At 11:57, a fresh Le Monde live-index item and the direct prefecture release clarified the Bordeaux-area evacuation picture: threatened sectors on Saint-Aubin-de-Medoc, Saint-Medard-en-Jalles, Martignas-sur-Jalle and Saint-Jean-d’Illac are under immediate evacuation, alongside Le Haillan, the outer-ring sectors of Merignac and Eysines, and the later Bruges extra-rocade notice. Treat these as exact FR-Alert/local-authority boundaries, not proof that every address in every named commune is covered. People are directed by road toward Bordeaux, with reception at Hall 3 of the Parc des Expositions. Earlier Le Monde entries reported 42 firefighters treated, 500 additional military personnel, 1.5 million FFP2 masks, nearly 20,000 hectares burned, about 140,000 evacuations and a government request to deploy a military A400M as soon as Saturday. The whole Cap Ferret peninsula remains evacuated and inbound road access remains unavailable. The maritime prefecture has partly reopened Arcachon Bay away from the fire, but the west-coast corridor and embarkation points remain restricted, evacuation boats are suspended, and this is not a public route to Cap Ferret. Bordeaux Airport’s official board is still listing flights and says airport activity continues, but tram and bus service is interrupted between Merignac Soleil and the airport.

What matters now
  • Newest clarification: additional Bordeaux-area evacuation orders apply by exact sector or local-authority boundary; do not infer that every named commune is fully evacuated unless your FR-Alert/local notice says so.
  • Le Monde reports that the government wants to deploy a military A400M for the Gironde fire as soon as Saturday; this is a response-resource signal, not confirmation of active aircraft operations.
  • Le Monde now reports 42 firefighters treated, including nine evacuated, all in relative emergencies.
  • Le Monde now reports nearly 20,000 hectares burned and about 140,000 evacuations, while describing firefighters as overwhelmed by the uncontrolled fire.
  • Road entry to Cap Ferret remains unavailable. Arcachon Bay is partly reopened away from the fire, but restricted west-coast/embarkation zones and the peninsula evacuation mean there is still no viable public-water route for a holiday trip.
  • Bordeaux Airport is still operating flights, but its official board says tram and bus service is interrupted between Merignac Soleil and the airport; verify the airport, airline and ground route before relying on any flight.
Assessment confidence

High confidence in the additional metropolitan evacuation orders and sectors: the prefecture confirms threatened sectors on Saint-Aubin-de-Medoc, Saint-Medard-en-Jalles, Martignas-sur-Jalle and Saint-Jean-d’Illac, separate notices cover Le Haillan, Eysines extra-rocade, Merignac extra-rocade and Bruges extra-rocade, and Le Monde reports the broader seven-commune/sector evacuation picture. Treat commune-level names as evacuation geography, not proof that every address in each named commune is covered; residents should follow their FR-Alert and local authority instructions. The exact operational boundary around the airport site still has not been established here. The airport board remains active and explicitly says activity continues, so the dashboard does not declare the airport closed; it treats ground access as disrupted because the same official board says tram and bus service to the airport is interrupted.
