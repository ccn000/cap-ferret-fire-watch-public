Current assessment

What we think now and why

Latest new information · 25 Jul 2026, 14:54

DeterioratingOpen assessmentClose assessment

At 14:54 CEST, Le Monde’s live index said the Saumos fire remains dangerous about 30 kilometres from Bordeaux and that 167,000 people have been evacuated in the South-West since Wednesday. It reports the Interior Minister saying the government is working through all scenarios, including a Bordeaux evacuation, while Bordeaux mayor Thomas Cazenave says evacuation of the city is not currently planned. The 13:00 Gironde prefecture point remains the latest direct official situation point found in this audit: nearly 32,700 hectares burned by noon, 57,000 overnight preventive evacuations in the listed Gironde sectors, 750 firefighters, 18 aircraft, 1,000 military support means and 1,700 security-force personnel. The Cap Ferret conclusion is unchanged: the peninsula remains evacuated, inbound public access is unavailable, and Lège-Cap-Ferret town hall still says not to return. Bordeaux Airport’s official board still lists flights and says airport activity continues; tram and bus access between Merignac Soleil and the airport remains interrupted, so terminal operation, flight operation and ground access remain separate assessments.

What matters now
  • Newest material update: Le Monde says the government is working through scenarios including a Bordeaux evacuation, while the Bordeaux mayor says evacuation of the city is not currently planned.
  • The Gironde prefecture says the Saumos fire had burned nearly 32,700 hectares by noon on 25 July and remains dangerous even though it is less virulent and no longer convective for the moment.
  • The prefecture reports 57,000 overnight preventive evacuations and 167,000 total preventive evacuations in Gironde; follow FR-Alert and local boundaries rather than assuming every address in every named commune is covered.
  • The response now includes 750 firefighters, 18 aircraft, 500 military already supporting firefighters, another 500 being added, and 1,700 security-force personnel protecting evacuated sectors.
  • Road entry to Cap Ferret remains unavailable. Arcachon Bay is partly reopened away from the fire, but restricted west-coast/embarkation zones and the peninsula evacuation mean there is still no viable public-water route for a holiday trip.
  • Bordeaux Airport is still operating flights, but its official board says tram and bus service is interrupted between Merignac Soleil and the airport; verify the airport, airline and ground route before relying on any flight.
Assessment confidence

High confidence in the 13:00 fire-scale and evacuation-scale figures because they come from the Gironde prefecture’s direct situation point and are independently surfaced by Le Monde’s live coverage. The newer 14:54 Le Monde live-index item is treated as evacuation-context evidence: official scenario planning for Bordeaux is not an evacuation order, and the city mayor says evacuation is not currently planned. Lège-Cap-Ferret town hall continues to tell people not to return to the commune. Treat commune names and extra-rocade wording as evacuation geography governed by FR-Alert and local boundaries, not proof that every address in every named commune is covered. The airport board remains active and explicitly says activity continues, so the dashboard does not declare the airport closed; it treats ground access as disrupted because the same official board says tram and bus service to the airport is interrupted.
