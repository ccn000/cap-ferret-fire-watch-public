Current assessment

What we think now and why

Latest new information · 26 Jul 2026, 19:30

Still worsening regionallyOpen assessmentClose assessment

The latest reviewed local signal is TVCapFerret’s 19:30 CEST summary of Mayor Philippe de Gonneville’s update: the fire remains at the gates of Lège-Cap-Ferret and is still not mastered; firebreaks around Claouey, Lège and La Pignada have been built or reinforced; patrols by volunteers, elected officials and municipal police continue; homes and domestic animals are being checked; and 700-800 firefighters are being hosted in Lège as a key logistics point. The broader official baseline remains the Gironde prefecture’s 13:00 situation point: 42,000 hectares burned, nearly 220,000 people preventively evacuated in 23 communes, 75 firefighters treated, 240 homes destroyed, 2,500 firefighters, 18 aircraft, 1,500 military personnel, 1,200 police/gendarmes and renewed A400M engagement. For Cap Ferret, the practical conclusion is unchanged: the peninsula remains evacuated, inbound public access is unavailable, road reopening and essential commerce have not been confirmed, and there is no tourist reopening signal.

What matters now
  • Newest material update: the Lège-Cap-Ferret mayor says the fire remains at the commune gates, is still not mastered, and no one should return until mastery is achieved.
  • Local defensive work continues: firebreaks around Claouey, Lège and La Pignada have been built or reinforced, while patrols and home/animal checks continue.
  • Lège is hosting 700-800 firefighters as a logistics point for the response.
  • The broader official baseline remains severe: the Gironde prefecture reports 42,000 hectares burned and about 220,000 preventive evacuations in 23 communes.
  • A63 and departmental road closures reinforce that road access and transit remain disrupted beyond the evacuated peninsula.
  • The Cap Ferret decision remains negative: the peninsula is evacuated, public inbound access is unavailable and no normal tourist reopening has been announced.
Assessment confidence

High confidence that the Cap Ferret practical reopening signal remains negative: the evacuation and no-return instructions have not been lifted, Inforoutes continues to warn against travel to Saumos, Le Porge and Lège-Cap-Ferret, and the 19:30 Lège-Cap-Ferret mayor point says the fire remains at the commune gates, is still not mastered and no return should happen until mastery is achieved. High confidence in the 42,000-hectare / about 220,000-evacuee / 240-home figures because they come from the Gironde prefecture. High confidence that Bordeaux Airport flight operation remains separate from ground-access risk because the official board continues to list arrivals and departures while publishing a separate hotel-zone evacuation and tram-stop disruption notice.
