Current assessment

What we think now and why

Latest new information · 25 Jul 2026, 23:47

Still dangerousOpen assessmentClose assessment

At 23:47 CEST, Le Monde’s live index reported 8,000 new evacuations ordered near Bordeaux after renewed fire activity, affecting several neighbourhoods of Marcheprime and Cestas. Direct official corroboration is partial in the pages checked this run: Marcheprime town hall confirms an evacuation-imminent local alert, while the checked Gironde prefecture press pages still show the 21:00 baseline rather than the newer Cestas/Marcheprime order. That 21:00 prefecture point remains the direct official consolidation: the Saumos fire is active and not fixed, changed direction twice during the day and is moving west-east; cooler, more humid conditions made it less virulent and allowed renewed offensive firefighting, but it remains dangerous. The prefecture keeps the local figures at nearly 32,700 hectares burned and 167,000 preventive evacuations in Gironde, with 68 firefighters treated, 10 evacuated, 140 homes destroyed, 18 aircraft engaged and the first A400M retardant drop completed. The Cap Ferret conclusion is unchanged: the peninsula remains evacuated, inbound public access is unavailable, and Lège-Cap-Ferret town hall still says not to return. Bordeaux Airport’s official board still lists arrivals and departures and says airport activity continues; tram stops between Merignac Soleil and the airport are not being served, so terminal operation, flight operation and ground access remain separate assessments.

What matters now
  • Newest material update: Le Monde reports 8,000 additional evacuations around Marcheprime and Cestas after renewed fire activity; direct official corroboration was partial in this run.
  • The prefecture’s direct 21:00 point remains the official baseline: the fire is active, not fixed and dangerous, with nearly 32,700 hectares burned and 167,000 preventive evacuations in Gironde.
  • No-return remains the key practical rule: evacuees cannot reintegrate while the fire is not fixed, and Lège-Cap-Ferret still says not to return.
  • Fire behaviour improved somewhat in the afternoon as temperatures fell and humidity rose, allowing firefighters to resume offensive action, but the fire changed direction twice and remains dangerous.
  • Road entry to Cap Ferret remains unavailable. Inforoutes now shows closures around Marcheprime, Cestas and the Bordeaux-to-Lège corridor.
  • Bordeaux Airport is still operating flights, but its official board says the tram is not stopping between Merignac Soleil and the airport; verify the airport, airline and ground route before relying on any flight.
