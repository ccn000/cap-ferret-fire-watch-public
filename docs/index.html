Gironde fire broadly stable overnight, still not mastered

The newest reviewed material signal is Le Monde’s 06:17 CEST live update: the Gironde fire remained “globally stable” overnight while Emmanuel Macron convenes an interministerial crisis cell. BFM reporting relayed through Google News says lighter wind and a small shower helped the Marcheprime/Cestas sector overnight. This improves the short-term fire-behaviour signal after Sunday evening’s worsening reports, but the wider official baseline remains severe: the Gironde prefecture’s 13:00 point reported 42,000 hectares burned, nearly 220,000 people preventively evacuated in 23 communes, 75 firefighters treated, 240 homes destroyed, major road closures including parts of the A63, and 2,500 firefighters with 18 aircraft plus military and police support. Cap Ferret remains evacuated and public inbound access is unavailable, but that is now treated as practical context rather than the headline.
