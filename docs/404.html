Situation report · Gironde

FULL EVACUATIONContained/stabilised but still watched · hotspots around Cap Ferret and Lacanau persist

Re-entry widens, hotspots remain

The situation is now materially better than yesterday: re-entry has expanded to Biganos and Audenge, Lacanau campsites and other tourist accommodation may reopen, and rail traffic south of Bordeaux is expected to resume progressively from Saturday. But this is not a clean all-clear. Le Monde’s current live index still flags several hotspots around Cap Ferret and Lacanau, and the larger Gironde fire remains a managed, staged-recovery emergency rather than a normal summer-access situation.

Current assessment

What we think now and why

Latest new information · 31 Jul 2026, 15:46

Improving staged recovery, with local hotspots still activeOpen assessmentClose assessment

The important update today is that staged return is broadening beyond yesterday’s first wave. Le Monde’s 15:46 live index says residents of Biganos and Audenge can return, adding to twelve communes already covered by re-entry measures, and that residents of Lacanau campsites, tourist residences and holiday villages are also authorised to come back. That is a real improvement for Bassin/Lacanau access and the tourism layer. At the same time, the same current live index says several hotspots persist around Cap Ferret and Lacanau, which keeps the operational picture fragile. Separate current reporting from Sud Ouest says rail traffic south of Bordeaux can resume progressively from Saturday. For Cap Ferret, the dashboard should now read as cautious improvement: better regional movement, but still wait for explicit local access and visitor instructions before treating the peninsula as normally open.
