Definitive current situation

Full assessment and confidence

Latest new information · 25 Jul 2026, 13:53

DeterioratingOpen assessmentClose assessment

At 13:00, the Gironde prefecture reported that the Saumos fire had burned nearly 32,700 hectares by noon. The prefecture says the fire is less virulent and no longer convective for the moment because of lower temperatures and higher humidity, but it still remains dangerous, continues to propagate and may face a wind shift during the afternoon. The same official point says 57,000 people were preventively evacuated overnight across Saint-Aubin-de-Medoc, Saint-Medard-en-Jalles, Martignas-sur-Jalle, Saint-Jean-d’Illac, Audenge, Le Haillan, Eysines extra-rocade and Merignac extra-rocade, bringing the Gironde total to 167,000 preventive evacuations. Response resources are now listed as 750 firefighters, 18 aircraft, 500 military already in support plus 500 more being added, and 1,700 security-force personnel. The Cap Ferret conclusion is unchanged: the peninsula remains evacuated, inbound public access is unavailable, and Lège-Cap-Ferret town hall still says not to return. Bordeaux Airport’s official board still lists flights, including BA2572, and says airport activity continues; tram and bus access between Merignac Soleil and the airport remains interrupted, so terminal operation, flight operation and ground access remain separate assessments.

What matters now
  • Newest material update: the Gironde prefecture says the Saumos fire had burned nearly 32,700 hectares by noon on 25 July and remains dangerous even though it is less virulent and no longer convective for the moment.
  • The prefecture reports 57,000 overnight preventive evacuations and 167,000 total preventive evacuations in Gironde; follow FR-Alert and local boundaries rather than assuming every address in every named commune is covered.
  • The response now includes 750 firefighters, 18 aircraft, 500 military already supporting firefighters, another 500 being added, and 1,700 security-force personnel protecting evacuated sectors.
  • The prefecture reports 54 firefighters injured and taken into care, including nine evacuated in relative emergency; no public reopening or return permission follows from this update.
  • Road entry to Cap Ferret remains unavailable. Arcachon Bay is partly reopened away from the fire, but restricted west-coast/embarkation zones and the peninsula evacuation mean there is still no viable public-water route for a holiday trip.
  • Bordeaux Airport is still operating flights, but its official board says tram and bus service is interrupted between Merignac Soleil and the airport; verify the airport, airline and ground route before relying on any flight.
Assessment confidence

High confidence in the updated fire-scale and evacuation-scale figures because the 32,700-hectare surface, 57,000 overnight preventive evacuations, 167,000 total preventive evacuations, listed response resources and firefighter-injury count come from the Gironde prefecture’s direct 13:00 situation point and are independently surfaced by Le Monde’s live coverage. Lège-Cap-Ferret town hall continues to tell people not to return to the commune. Treat commune names and extra-rocade wording as evacuation geography governed by FR-Alert and local boundaries, not proof that every address in every named commune is covered. The exact operational boundary around the airport site still has not been established here. The airport board remains active and explicitly says activity continues, so the dashboard does not declare the airport closed; it treats ground access as disrupted because the same official board says tram and bus service to the airport is interrupted.
