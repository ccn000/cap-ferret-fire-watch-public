FULL EVACUATIONStabilised after a very calm night · still not fixed/controlled

Re-entry begins, but fire still only stabilised

The latest material development is a real easing signal for the wider Gironde access picture: Le Monde’s live index reports that residents of nine communes, about 84,000 people, are authorised to return, firefighters are “reasonably optimistic”, and the A63 is due to reopen from 14:00. That is materially better than yesterday’s watch-and-wait posture. It still does not mean Cap Ferret is open to tourists: the fire has burned about 42,000 hectares, remains described as stabilised rather than fixed/controlled, and local access and evacuation orders still govern any movement toward the peninsula.
