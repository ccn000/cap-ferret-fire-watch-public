Definitive current situation

Full assessment and confidence

Latest new information · 25 Jul 2026, 06:30

DeterioratingOpen assessmentClose assessment

At 03:32 and 03:58, the warning became a set of evacuation orders. The prefect ordered five municipalities evacuated preventively and ordered partial evacuations in the outer-ring sectors of Mérignac and Eysines. People were directed by road toward Bordeaux, with reception at Hall 3 of the Parc des Expositions. At 04:09, Le Monde reported that 42 firefighters had been treated, including nine evacuated, all in relative emergencies. At 06:17, Le Monde’s live index said 500 additional military personnel and 1.5 million FFP2 masks are being sent to Gironde. At 06:30, Le Monde described firefighters as overwhelmed by an uncontrolled fire that has already caused about 140,000 evacuations and burned nearly 20,000 hectares. The whole Cap Ferret peninsula remains evacuated, inbound road access and ordinary navigation on Arcachon Bay remain unavailable, and no reopening signal exists. Bordeaux Airport’s official board is still listing flights, but the evacuation of outer Mérignac materially raises both closure and ground-access risk.

What matters now
  • Seven additional Bordeaux-area municipalities are now wholly or partly under preventive evacuation orders; this is no longer only a prepare-to-evacuate warning.
  • Le Monde now reports 42 firefighters treated, including nine evacuated, all in relative emergencies.
  • Le Monde now reports nearly 20,000 hectares burned and about 140,000 evacuations, while describing firefighters as overwhelmed by the uncontrolled fire.
  • Road entry and ordinary navigation on Arcachon Bay are unavailable, so there is no viable land or public-water route for a holiday trip.
  • Bordeaux Airport is still listing flights, but outer Mérignac is now under an evacuation order; verify the airport, airline and road access before relying on any flight.
Assessment confidence

High confidence in the seven additional metropolitan evacuation orders: Le Monde reports the prefectural instructions and AFP/ATS independently reports the same municipalities and partial scope. The exact boundary of the outer-Mérignac order and whether it formally includes the airport site have not been established here. The airport board remains active, so the dashboard does not declare the airport closed; it now treats access and continuity as materially uncertain rather than stable.

Correction: Bordeaux Airport remained open despite the minister’s closure statement
