Current assessment

What we think now and why

Latest new information · 25 Jul 2026, 21:06

DeterioratingOpen assessmentClose assessment

At 21:06 CEST, Le Monde reported that Marcheprime town hall had told residents to prepare to leave as the fire approached from Blagon; the town hall page itself calls this an extreme, evacuation-imminent alert and tells residents to prepare to head south toward Biganos or the motorway while awaiting prefectural confirmation. This is a new evacuation-scope warning in the Bordeaux approach corridor, not yet a reviewed prefectural evacuation order for Marcheprime. The earlier 20:41 national warning still stands: Prime Minister Sebastien Lecornu said the next hours may be difficult in Gironde and that the situation remains particularly critical. The direct Gironde prefecture point remains the local baseline at nearly 32,700 hectares burned and 167,000 preventive evacuations in Gironde; wider Gironde/Landes coverage remains around 200,000 evacuees. The Cap Ferret conclusion is unchanged: the peninsula remains evacuated, inbound public access is unavailable, and Lège-Cap-Ferret town hall still says not to return. Bordeaux Airport’s official board still lists arrivals and departures and says airport activity continues; tram stops between Merignac Soleil and the airport are not being served, so terminal operation, flight operation and ground access remain separate assessments.

What matters now
