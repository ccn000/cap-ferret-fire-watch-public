The short version

  1. The weighted historical median practical reopening in the current analogue set is 22 days after evacuation.
  2. The smoothed prior median is day 27; the posterior median is day 28. In this snapshot, the live evidence shifts the median by about 1 days, so most of the pessimism is coming from the base-rate/tail design rather than the live likelihood update alone.
  3. The current incident is coded as tourist/coastal/WUI/single-access, with ordinary public access still blocked, road access not confirmed and essential commerce not restarted.
  4. Those live blockers mainly suppress very early reopening dates. They matter most in the first 7–14 days; by the 3–4 week range their multipliers have mostly stopped penalising dates.
  5. The most important caveat is that this is still a 24-case structured library, not a 500-case fitted survival model. It is designed to be legible and revisable rather than statistically elegant.
